# M8 — One flow: the venue race

<!-- generated: scope agreed with the user on 2026-10-25 -->

**Status:** Done locally (C1 and C3 met; C2 browser-checked, with animation smoothness to rehearse in a visible window). Not yet deployed.

## Why

The user found the lab confusing: chat closure cases, a separate **Plan an event** entry and an **Event calendar** all competed, and none fed one narrative. Highlighted visitors were also invisible: they were the same small dots, mostly at home and off-screen.

## The one flow

1. **Pick an artist.** This can be a city artist or any artist, looked up live in Qloo.
2. **Shortlist.** Three existing Qloo venues that fit (`rankArtistVenues`) are lettered **A/B/C** on the map. Each has its own colour, and its recent visitors are lit in that colour.
3. **Race.** The three options play as three parallel futures from the same city and seed, one event each, night by night. The people who go to each event walk there in its colour, and a live tally climbs on each card.
4. **Result.** The race shows the winner and the turnout at each venue, plus how many of 3 seeded futures each venue won. It compares the venue's usual nights with its nights during the event. The night you watched is future 1 of 3.
5. **Tweak.** Change the night, the number of nights, the artist, or swap a venue, then race again.

The live city is never changed by a race. The chat stays as a collapsed helper that explains the current race. Closures, rent, openings, the calendar and the planner leave the visible UI.

## Pieces

- `sim/events.ts`: `PairedEventRun` runs one seeded no-event baseline plus one fork per candidate, a tick at a time. `runEventCandidates` uses it, so the race and the forecast are the same computation. Candidates may carry their own `hostVenue`.
- Worker: `startRace`, then `play`/`pause` on the `race` timeline. Race frames are the baseline city, with each event's attendees redirected to their venue. A `race` block carries the groups and tallies, and a `race-complete` message is sent at the end.
- Render: the `race` focus gives each candidate's people its colour and makes them larger, with others dimmed. Venues show coloured beacons and A/B/C pins.
- UI: `FlowShell` (new root) and `VenueRace` panel; old `GameShell` stays in source, unmounted.

## Outcome notes

- Catchment rings were dropped: a 1.5 km reach covers most of the demo area.
- Earlier nights' visitors stay faintly lit (`race.seen`), because a single night draws only 5–15 people per venue.
- The guide is a separate route (`/api/race-helper`) with no browser tools, so answers don't wait on browser round trips.
